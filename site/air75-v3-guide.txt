Which mode should I use?

Win / Mac: match your computer.

Wired: USB-C data cable. Wireless: Bluetooth or dongle. Off: power down.

How do I pair Bluetooth?

Select Wireless. Hold Fn + 1, 2 or 3 for 3 seconds, then pair in your computer's Bluetooth settings.

What does the knob do?

Turn to adjust volume. Press to mute.

Installation diagrams: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0268/7297/1373/files/Air75_V3_Quick_Guide_FAQ.pdf?v=1753438891#page=4

Can I remap keys?

USB + Wired, then NuPhy IO in Edge or Chrome. Customize keys, lighting and knob actions.

Open NuPhy IO: https://www.nuphy.io/

Still won't connect?

Dongle: hold Fn + 4 for 3 seconds, then reinsert it. Avoid metal USB hubs.

Bluetooth: forget the saved device and pair again. Check firmware in NuPhy IO if needed.

Contact NuPhy: mailto:service@nuphy.com
